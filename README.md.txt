# AI Ready School — Homepage Redesign

## Overview

This project is a redesign of the AI Ready School homepage. The goal was to improve clarity, usability, and conversion for the primary users — school principals and academic leaders who need to quickly understand the value of the product.

---

## Key Problems Identified

* The hero section was not clearly communicating the real value of the product. It described what the platform is, but not what it actually does for schools.

* The navigation had too many options, which made it difficult for users to quickly decide where to go.

* Product names were introduced before explaining what they do, which added unnecessary confusion for first-time visitors.

* Trust signals were weak and not placed effectively, making it harder for users to feel confident about the platform.

* The main call-to-action was not strong enough and was inconsistent across the page.

* It was not clear who the product is best suited for, making it harder for users to relate to the offering.

---

## What I Focused On

* Simplifying the navigation to reduce decision fatigue and make key paths clearer.

* Rewriting the hero section to clearly communicate the value and benefits within a few seconds.

* Structuring the product section using simple, role-based explanations before introducing product names.

* Improving the visibility and consistency of the call-to-action by using a single primary action (“Book Free Demo”).

* Bringing trust signals into the hero section so they are visible immediately.

* Keeping the design clean, minimal, and easy to scan.

---

## What I Would Improve With More Time

* Add real case studies with measurable outcomes to strengthen credibility.

* Include a clearer onboarding or “how it works” section to guide users.

* Introduce subtle animations and interactions to improve engagement.

* Improve accessibility further (keyboard navigation, ARIA enhancements, etc.).

* Explore different variations of the hero headline through A/B testing.

---

---

## Notes

This redesign focuses more on clarity and user experience rather than building a fully complete product. The intention was to solve the most important UX issues within the given time constraint.
